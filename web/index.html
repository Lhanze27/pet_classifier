What breed is it?

A fine-grained image classifier for domestic cats and dogs. Built on EfficientNetV2B0 with two-phase transfer learning on the Oxford-IIIT Pet dataset — trained to distinguish 37 breeds at 92.45% test accuracy.

A note on limits. This catalog recognizes thirty-seven breeds from the Oxford-IIIT Pet dataset — twelve cats, twenty-five dogs. Photographs of other animals, mixed breeds, or breeds outside this list will still receive a verdict; consult the confidence score and the list below before drawing conclusions.
